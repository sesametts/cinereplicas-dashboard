Alert

November is running 49% below the same sixteen days in October: €459.04 against €892.94, on half the audience — impressions down 49%, clicks down 66%.

The offers that opened on November 11 did lift the pace, from €24.64 a day over the first ten days to €35.44 since. But the month is tracking to about €861, a third of the €2,600 target, and closing it would take €153 a day over the fourteen days left.

Advertising is the sharper problem: €365.83 spent for €459.04 of shop revenue. ROAS stands at 1.84 against a 7.2 break-even.

Where we stand

Conversion is what works. 1.06% of clicks become an order against 0.62% in October, and 1.59% over the last seven days. The shop sells better to the people it reaches — it simply reaches far fewer of them.

The average order has fallen to €16.39 from €18.22, and to €13.29 over the last seven days: the offers move more units at a lower price, which is working as intended but changes what advertising can afford.

94% of revenue still comes from affiliate content, and one product carries the shop: the Retinol + Hyaluronic Acid Night Cream 50 ml.

Next steps

  • Ship the samples approved for November and December — content is what moves this shop, not paid reach.
  • Decide together whether the €2,600 November target still holds, given the pace since the offers opened.
  • Reconfirm the 7.2 break-even if the selling price moves for Black Friday.
  • Advertising is under review this week: at the current average order it does not pay for itself.
  • File the German EPR number to reopen Germany.
